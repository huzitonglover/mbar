系统要求

macOS 15 或更高版本，支持 Apple 芯片和 Intel 芯片的 Mac。macOS 27.2 起系统自带菜单栏图标管理，mBar 在这些系统上不隐藏图标。

基本用法

  • mBar 会在菜单栏放一个小图标：它右边的图标正常显示，左边的图标会被收起。
  • 按住 ⌘ 拖动任意菜单栏图标，就能决定它放在 mBar 图标的哪一边。
  • 点 mBar 图标展开收起的图标，再点一次收起。也可以按快捷键（默认 ⌃⌥B），或在带刘海的屏幕上把鼠标停在刘海旁的空白处。
  • 在主窗口的「自动显示」里勾选「连接外接显示器时」或「接通电源时」，条件满足期间会一直显示全部图标。
  • 右键点 mBar 图标可以打开主窗口；再次打开 mBar App 也会显示主窗口。

常见问题

mBar 需要哪些权限？
不需要任何系统权限。mBar 只控制它自己的菜单栏图标，其他图标的位置由你按住 ⌘ 拖动决定。

悬停或点击菜单栏空白处没有反应？
这个功能只在带刘海的屏幕上可用，范围是刘海和右侧图标之间的空白处。没有刘海的屏幕上请点 mBar 图标或按快捷键。

图标位置乱了或互相抢位置？
请不要同时运行其他菜单栏图标管理工具，它们的工作方式相同，会互相影响。退出其他工具后重新打开 mBar 即可。

怎么卸载？
退出 mBar（右键 mBar 图标 → 退出），把「应用程序」里的 mBar 移到废纸篓。

Requirements

macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel Macs. Starting with macOS 27.2, macOS manages menu bar icons itself, and mBar does not hide icons there.

Getting started

  • mBar adds a small icon to the menu bar. Icons to its right stay visible; icons to its left are tucked away.
  • Hold Command and drag any menu bar icon to choose which side of the mBar icon it goes on.
  • Click the mBar icon to show tucked-away icons, and click again to hide them. You can also press the shortcut (Control-Option-B by default) or, on a display with a notch, point at the empty space beside the notch.
  • Under Show Automatically in the main window, check “External display connected” or “On power adapter” to keep all icons visible while that condition is met.
  • Right-click the mBar icon to open the main window, or open the mBar app again.

FAQ

Which permissions does mBar need?
None. mBar only controls its own menu bar item. You decide where other icons go by Command-dragging them.

Hovering over or clicking the menu bar does nothing.
This works only on displays with a notch, in the empty space between the notch and the icons on the right. On other displays, click the mBar icon or press the shortcut.

Icons keep moving or fighting for position.
Please don't run another menu bar icon manager at the same time; they work the same way and interfere with each other. Quit the other tool and reopen mBar.

How do I uninstall mBar?
Quit mBar (right-click the mBar icon → Quit) and move mBar from Applications to the Trash.
