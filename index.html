系统要求

macOS 15 或更高版本，支持 Apple 芯片和 Intel 芯片的 Mac。

基本用法

  • mBar 会在菜单栏放一个开关图标：开关右边的图标正常显示，左边的图标会被收起。
  • 普通模式：点开关展开全部隐藏的图标，再点一次收起。这个模式不需要任何权限。
  • 聚合模式：点开关弹出面板，隐藏的图标集中显示在里面，点图标即可打开它原本的菜单。
  • 在主窗口的「菜单栏图标」页，给每个图标选择 显示 / 隐藏 / 始终隐藏，mBar 会自动移动它。也可以按住 ⌘ 直接拖动图标。
  • 右键点开关可以打开主窗口；再次打开 mBar App 也会显示主窗口。

常见问题

为什么需要辅助功能和屏幕录制权限？
聚合模式和自动移动图标需要这两项权限：辅助功能用来移动图标、在面板里打开图标的菜单；屏幕录制只用来截取菜单栏图标显示在面板里。截图只在本机内存中使用，不会保存或上传。只用普通模式时不需要任何权限。

怎么授权？
打开 系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 辅助功能 / 屏幕录制，打开 mBar 右边的开关。列表里没有 mBar 时，点下方的「+」把 mBar 添加进去。授权屏幕录制后可能需要重新打开 mBar。

开关已经打开，但 mBar 仍提示没有权限？
在系统设置的列表里选中 mBar，点「−」删除，再重新添加并打开开关，然后重新打开 mBar。

面板里看不到某个图标，或显示的是 App 图标？
被刘海挡住、系统放不下的图标可能截不到，mBar 会用 App 图标代替，点击仍然可以打开它的菜单。也可以把这个图标设为「显示」。

图标位置乱了或互相抢位置？
请不要同时运行其他菜单栏图标管理工具，它们的工作方式相同，会互相影响。退出其他工具后重新打开 mBar 即可。

怎么卸载？
退出 mBar（右键开关 → 退出），把「应用程序」里的 mBar 移到废纸篓。需要的话，在系统设置里删除它的辅助功能和屏幕录制授权。

Requirements

macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel Macs.

Getting started

  • mBar adds a toggle to the menu bar. Icons to the right of the toggle stay visible; icons to its left are hidden.
  • Normal Mode: click the toggle to reveal all hidden icons, click again to hide them. No permissions required.
  • Advanced Mode: clicking the toggle opens a panel with your hidden icons. Click one to open its menu.
  • On the Menu Bar Icons page of the main window, choose Shown / Hidden / Always Hidden for each icon and mBar moves it for you. You can also ⌘-drag icons yourself.
  • Right-click the toggle to open the main window, or open the mBar app again.

FAQ

Why does mBar need Accessibility and Screen Recording?
Only Advanced Mode and automatic icon moving need them. Accessibility is used to move icons and open an icon's menu from the panel. Screen Recording is used only to capture images of menu bar icons for the panel. These images stay in memory on your Mac and are never saved or uploaded. Normal Mode needs no permissions.

How do I grant the permissions?
Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility / Screen Recording and turn on mBar. If mBar isn't listed, click “+” and add it. You may need to reopen mBar after granting Screen Recording.

The switch is on, but mBar still says it has no permission.
Select mBar in the System Settings list, click “−” to remove it, add it again, turn it on, then reopen mBar.

An icon is missing from the panel, or shows the app icon instead.
Icons hidden behind the notch that macOS can't place may not be capturable, so mBar shows the app icon instead. Clicking it still opens the menu. You can also set that icon to Shown.

Icons keep moving or fighting for position.
Please don't run another menu bar icon manager at the same time; they work the same way and interfere with each other. Quit the other tool and reopen mBar.

How do I uninstall mBar?
Quit mBar (right-click the toggle → Quit) and move mBar from Applications to the Trash. If you like, remove its Accessibility and Screen Recording entries in System Settings.
