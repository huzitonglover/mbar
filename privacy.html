概述

mBar（以下简称「本 App」）由 Hangzhou Yimi Cultural and Creative Co., Ltd.（以下简称「我们」）开发，是一款 macOS 菜单栏图标整理工具。我们重视你的隐私：本 App 不联网，不收集、不上传、不分享任何个人数据。

我们不收集的信息

本 App 不包含任何网络请求代码，也不集成任何第三方统计、广告或崩溃收集 SDK。我们不会收集你的姓名、联系方式、设备标识、位置、使用数据或任何其他信息。

本 App 在你的 Mac 上处理的信息

  • 设置：你选择的模式、自动收起时间、哪些图标设为「始终隐藏」（以 App 的标识记录）、每个 App 图标适用的点击方式等。这些设置保存在本 App 的沙盒容器里，只存在于你的 Mac 上，删除本 App 后可一并删除。
  • 菜单栏图标截图：授予屏幕录制权限后，本 App 会截取菜单栏图标的图像，显示在聚合面板和图标列表里。这些图像只保存在内存中，不会写入磁盘，也不会离开你的 Mac。
  • 菜单栏图标的位置和所属 App：本 App 读取菜单栏图标窗口的位置和名称，用来判断图标属于哪个 App、应该显示还是隐藏。这些信息只在运行时使用，不会保存或上传。

系统权限

  • 辅助功能：用于移动菜单栏图标、在聚合面板里打开图标的菜单。
  • 屏幕录制：只用于截取菜单栏图标的图像，不会录制或截取屏幕上的其他内容。

这两项权限都可以随时在 系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 中关闭。只使用普通模式时不需要任何权限。

儿童隐私

本 App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁以下儿童的信息。

政策变更

如果本政策有更新，我们会在本页面发布新版本并更新生效日期。如果本 App 将来增加任何数据收集，我们会事先在本页面和 App Store 的隐私信息中说明。

Overview

mBar (“the App”) is a macOS menu bar icon organizer developed by Hangzhou Yimi Cultural and Creative Co., Ltd. (“we”). We respect your privacy: the App does not connect to the internet and does not collect, upload or share any personal data.

Information we do not collect

The App contains no networking code and no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not collect your name, contact details, device identifiers, location, usage data or any other information.

Information the App handles on your Mac

  • Settings: your chosen mode, auto-hide delay, which icons are set to Always Hidden (recorded by app identifier), the click method that works for each app's icon, and similar preferences. They are stored in the App's sandbox container on your Mac only, and can be removed together with the App.
  • Images of menu bar icons: with Screen Recording permission, the App captures images of menu bar icons to show them in the panel and the icon list. These images are kept in memory only, never written to disk and never leave your Mac.
  • Menu bar icon positions and owners: the App reads the position and name of menu bar icon windows to work out which app each icon belongs to and whether it should be shown or hidden. This information is used only while the App is running and is never saved or uploaded.

System permissions

  • Accessibility: used to move menu bar icons and to open an icon's menu from the panel.
  • Screen Recording: used only to capture images of menu bar icons. The App does not record or capture anything else on your screen.

You can turn off either permission at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Normal Mode needs no permissions at all.

Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. If the App ever starts collecting data, we will say so here and in the App Store privacy details beforehand.
