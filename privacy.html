概述

mBar（以下简称「本 App」）由 Hangzhou Yimi Cultural and Creative Co., Ltd.（以下简称「我们」）开发，是一款 macOS 菜单栏图标整理工具。我们重视你的隐私：本 App 不联网，不收集、不上传、不分享任何个人数据。

我们不收集的信息

本 App 不包含任何网络请求代码，也不集成任何第三方统计、广告或崩溃收集 SDK。我们不会收集你的姓名、联系方式、设备标识、位置、使用数据或任何其他信息。

本 App 在你的 Mac 上处理的信息

  • 设置：自动收起时间、快捷键、展开方式、自动显示的条件、语言等。这些设置保存在本 App 的沙盒容器里，只存在于你的 Mac 上，删除本 App 后可一并删除。
  • 菜单栏图标的位置：本 App 读取菜单栏上图标窗口的位置和大小（不读取标题或内容），用来判断鼠标是否停在菜单栏空白处。这些信息只在运行时使用，不会保存或上传。
  • 显示器和电源状态：本 App 读取是否连接了外接显示器、是否接通电源，用于「自动显示」。这些信息只在运行时使用，不会保存或上传。

系统权限

本 App 不需要、也不会请求任何系统权限，包括辅助功能、屏幕录制和自动化。

儿童隐私

本 App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁以下儿童的信息。

政策变更

如果本政策有更新，我们会在本页面发布新版本并更新生效日期。如果本 App 将来增加任何数据收集，我们会事先在本页面和 App Store 的隐私信息中说明。

Overview

mBar (“the App”) is a macOS menu bar icon organizer developed by Hangzhou Yimi Cultural and Creative Co., Ltd. (“we”). We respect your privacy: the App does not connect to the internet and does not collect, upload or share any personal data.

Information we do not collect

The App contains no networking code and no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not collect your name, contact details, device identifiers, location, usage data or any other information.

Information the App handles on your Mac

  • Settings: auto-hide delay, shortcut, how icons are shown, the Show Automatically conditions, language and similar preferences. They are stored in the App's sandbox container on your Mac only, and can be removed together with the App.
  • Menu bar icon positions: the App reads the position and size of menu bar icon windows (not their titles or contents) to tell when the pointer is over an empty part of the menu bar. This information is used only while the App is running and is never saved or uploaded.
  • Display and power status: the App checks whether an external display is connected and whether the Mac is on power, for Show Automatically. This information is used only while the App is running and is never saved or uploaded.

System permissions

The App does not need or request any system permissions, including Accessibility, Screen Recording and Automation.

Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date. If the App ever starts collecting data, we will say so here and in the App Store privacy details beforehand.
